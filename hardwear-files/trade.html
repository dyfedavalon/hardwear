Trade questions

The things buyers always ask.

Can I see one before I commit?

Yes. We'll post a plain sample free to any UK address, and a stitched sample with your own logo on it once artwork is approved — also free. Nothing goes into production until you've had one in your hands.

What artwork do you need?

A vector file is ideal — .ai, .eps, .svg or a vector PDF. We can work from a high-resolution PNG if that's all you have, but very fine detail and thin type don't stitch well at 40 mm and we'll say so up front rather than after.

Do you do hi-vis for site compliance?

The Hi-Vis Amber colourway has reflective piping along the panel seams. It is not certified PPE and does not replace a compliant vest or helmet — treat it as a visible cap rather than safety equipment.

What are the payment terms?

First order is paid up front. After that we'll open a 30-day account on approved credit for orders over £1,000. All prices on this page exclude VAT.

Can we mix sizes?

There's only one size — 54 to 61 cm, set by the webbing adjuster. It's one of the reasons bulk ordering is straightforward: no guessing at a size split for forty people.

What if some don't fit or arrive faulty?

Every unit carries the same 90-day guarantee as a retail cap. Faulty or ill-fitting units get replaced individually — you don't have to send the whole order back.
