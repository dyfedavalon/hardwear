The fabric

Canvas on the outside. Sportswear on the inside.

A washed cotton-nylon ripstop canvas gives it the weight and the ageing of proper workwear. A wicking mesh bonded to the back of it stops the canvas doing what canvas always does — holding your sweat until it stains.

The result is a cap that stays the same colour wet or dry. No dark crescent above the peak. No salt line come Friday.

Where it comes from

Written down in Wrexham. Built by people who know how.

Ashley Roberts spent fourteen years on the tools, binning caps that went stiff, stank or bleached out by August. He kept a list of how each one died, and eventually turned it into a two-page specification for the cap nobody was selling.

We don't own a factory. We own that specification, and we check every batch against it before it goes anywhere near you.
